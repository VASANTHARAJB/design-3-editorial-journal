Journal Policies

Standards we follow for quality and integrity

Peer Review Policy

All manuscripts undergo editorial screening followed by double-blind review by at least two independent experts. Decisions are based on originality, methodology, clarity and contribution.

Publication Ethics

We follow COPE guidelines. Authors must ensure honesty, proper authorship, disclosure of conflicts of interest and ethical research conduct.

Plagiarism Policy

Every submission is screened with similarity-detection software. Manuscripts with unacceptable similarity are rejected without review.

Open Access and Copyright

Published articles are freely available worldwide. Authors retain copyright and grant the journal a licence to publish under Creative Commons CC BY 4.0.

Author Guidelines

Submit your manuscript in MS Word format with a 250-word abstract, 4-6 keywords, APA-style references and a cover letter.

Privacy Policy

Personal data collected is used only for editorial and communication purposes and is never sold or shared with third parties.
